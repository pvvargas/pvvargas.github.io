03 / The person behind the words

A writer with two toolkits.

I'm Paulo, a Philippines-based technology writer and senior technical writer. I like taking things apart intellectually, whether that's a new gadget, a sweeping AI claim, or a tangled help center.

My editorial work spans consumer technology, reviews, features, and opinion. My technical writing background brings a different discipline: thinking about the reader's next question, building useful information structures, and making complex workflows feel straightforward.

The common thread is curiosity, precision, and a low tolerance for empty hype.
